THE SET

Story in the first two: yes. Image 1 says what drip does (one video, tokens) and image 2 shows the consequence (the lock). A shopper gets the product from those two. Image 3 would be a fair third because it shows the mode range.

Consistency: the caption panel is solid. Same width, same mark position, same headline baseline, every headline and subline two lines. The strips are not consistent. Top inset runs from about 42px (images 1 and 5) to 100px (image 3), image 1's content is narrower than the rest, and image 3 leaves about 120px of empty black at the bottom while 4 and 5 run full height. Note counts vary (image 1 has one note plus a legend, the rest two), which is fine. Image 2's water band is the only frame decoration in the set, so it has to be right or go.

Thumbnail legibility: the serif headlines read at thumbnail size in all five. Notes and most strip text don't, which is expected. Images 1 (illustration), 2 (white button on dark card), and 5 (blue bars) keep a shape you can recognize. Image 4 becomes rows of grey text and blue toggles, and image 3 three grey cards.

Copy flags: "Customize picks which limits are on." misreads. "Your video pauses at the daily limit, if you set one." ends on a hedge. "Easy to tighten. Slow to loosen." is a parallel slogan of the kind AI copy reaches for, but it's specific and true here, so keep it. Nothing else reads as generic.

Rejection or misleading risk: low. No YouTube logo or red play mark appears. "YouTube" in captions is descriptive use. The home strip copies YouTube's dark styling (duration badge, kebab menu), but it's drip's own page and carries no YouTube branding. Numbers agree across images (1h a day, 3 unlocks a week, 15-minute drops). Image 2's water that doesn't read as water is the closest thing to misleading, because the note promises something the picture doesn't show.

Top 5 fixes:
1. Image 2: one visible waterline across the strip at the same height as the panel band, with the second leader on it. Or remove the band.
2. Image 1: end the leader on the "Reveal next" button, not the gap above it.
3. Image 3: repoint "Switch freely for the first 24 hours." to the strip subline, and rewrite "Customize picks which limits are on."
4. Image 4: shift the crop down about 60px so the first note clears the subline, and replace the Dry hours note with one about tightening.
5. Tiles: use c2, or rework c3 so the card shows clearly and clears the text by 16px or more. Also even out strip top insets and fill image 3's empty bottom.
