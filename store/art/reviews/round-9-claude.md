THE SET

Story in the first two: yes. Screenshot 1 says what it does (one video, tokens), screenshot 2 says what happens at the limit. Someone who sees only the first two understands the product. Screenshot 2 is the weaker of the pair because the water doesn't read.

Consistency: the left columns are consistent (logo, caption, subline at the same positions, captions all two lines, sublines all two lines). The right strips are not. Margins run from about 23px (3) through 38px (4) to 60px (1). Screenshot 5's background color and rounded corner differ from the rest. Vertical fill varies: 4 has large black bands top and bottom, 3 has one at the bottom, 1 and 5 are full. Note positions are set by their targets, which is right, but 1 and 4 leave large empty patches in the left column. Leader targeting varies: 4 is precise, 3 points at card borders, 5 has a kink and a dot inside a tile, 2 points at empty water.

Thumbnail legibility: headlines read at quarter size. Sublines and notes don't, which is expected. Right strips: 1 (the thumbnail) and 5 (the bar chart) read best, 3 and 4 read as generic dark UI lists, and 2 reads as a black box on a dark blur. Since all five left columns look identical in the carousel strip, the right side does all the work of telling frames apart.

AI look across the set: the main tell is the template itself. A warm beige panel, a high-contrast serif headline, a black product panel, and thin blue leaders ending in dots all show up in a lot of generated landing pages right now. The copy is plainer and more specific than typical AI copy. One detail of drip's own breaking the template on one or two frames would do more than any copy change. For example, let screenshot 2's waterline continue across into the beige column, or draw the drop/card legend icons at a larger, illustrated size like the thumbnail.

Copy flags:
- "Limits are optional." (2) reads defensive and generic.
- "Blue water fills the screen" (2) doesn't match the teal shown.
- "Dry hours: drops can't be..." (4) is label-colon form.
- "Curated keeps one at a time, with no daily limit." (3) repeats the UI.
- "Stash it, or pass. Each new pick uses a card." (1) is fine as copy but mismatched to its target.
Nothing else reads as AI written. "Easy to tighten. Slow to loosen." and "Down 7 minutes a day from the week before." are the best lines.

Rejection or misleading risk: low. No YouTube logo or real thumbnails. "YouTube" appears as text in screenshot 1's subline and screenshot 3's UI, which is nominative and normally accepted. Keep it out of the tiles, which you have. The Watch button's tilted play glyph in a white pill is close enough to YouTube's play mark that a strict reviewer could ask, but it's drip's own UI, so it's not fixable here and probably fine. The "1.2M views · 1w ago" metadata mimics YouTube's format with fictional data, which is fine. Claims match the product as described.

Top 5 fixes across the set:
1. Screenshot 5: make the strip near black and square like the others (no gray card, no rounded corner), straighten the first leader, and move the second dot off the Drops tile onto the selected Saturday column.
2. Screenshot 2: stop the water with a visible surface line and point the second leader at it. Drop "Blue" from the note unless the water reads blue. Remove the bubble clipped behind the card.
3. Screenshot 3: scale the welcome page to screenshot 4's margins, move the Customize dot onto the link, and replace or cut the note that repeats the Curated card.
4. Screenshot 1: retarget the leader to "Reveal next · -1" with matching copy, and rebalance the empty band between the legend and the note.
5. Tiles: ship c2, align the drop to the text block, and give the drop one drip-specific detail. Screenshot 4: scale up about 8 to 10 percent to fill the black bands.
