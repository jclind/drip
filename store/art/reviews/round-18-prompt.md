You are one judge on a panel choosing between three designs for a set of Chrome Web Store listing images. Do not edit or create any files. Report only. Judge only what you see in the attached images; you are not told any earlier scores or what other judges think.

## The product

drip is a Chrome extension that replaces YouTube's endless home feed with a search box or one recommended video at a time, with optional daily watch-time limits. Its tokens are a blue cartoon "drop" (15 minutes of watching, by default) and a gold cartoon "card" (reveals one new video). When the minutes run out, a lock comes up over the video with blue water rising. The tone it aims for: a little character without being distracting; restraint should feel good.

## The three sets, attached in this order

Each set has five 1280x800 screenshots and one 440x280 promo tile, showing the same five pages of the real extension (rendered from the real code, with sample data): 1 the home page with one revealed video (a drawn thumbnail, on purpose), 2 the lock screen, 3 the welcome page's mode choice, 4 Settings with a raised limit waiting for the daily reset, 5 History (a labeled sample week).

- Set V (v-screenshot-1.png to v-screenshot-5.png, v-tile.png): a cream caption panel on the left with a serif headline, a subline, and notes joined to the page by thin blue leader lines; the page as a full-height dark strip on the right.
- Set A (a-screenshot-1.png to a-screenshot-5.png, a-tile.png): one huge serif numeral per image with a blue drop as its full stop and a short phrase; the page as a slab from the top right; a blue ring on the place in the page that holds the number.
- Set B (b-screenshot-1.png to b-screenshot-5.png, b-tile.png): flat blue, gold, or ink grounds, one huge symbol per image built around or behind the page, short bold text, no notes.

The pages themselves can't change; only the framing, captions, crop and scale, drawing, and sample data can. Claims must match the product: limits are optional, a looser setting waits for the daily reset after the first day, a tighter one applies at once, sample data is labeled.
