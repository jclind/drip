The median design score is 7.

The best ones (Adobe 08 and 09, NordPass 11 to 13) make one claim per frame and show one piece of real UI or photo at a size you can read. Every frame in the set follows one system: NordPass has its teal, its pill tag and its fingerprint lines, and Adobe has gray with a bold-plus-light headline. Nothing decorates. The weaker ones (Monica 01 to 05, 03 and 07 in particular) cram three panels or floating chips into one frame and shrink UI until you can't read it. They add 3D sparkles, pushpins and light bulbs, write hype copy ("Unleash", "stunning"), and fill frames with AI-generated imagery, and that is most of their AI look. 10 also has a typo ("FIle Type") and a checkerboard pasted over the subject that confuses more than it explains.

Text load: Adobe carries 4 or 5 words, headline only. NordPass carries about 15 to 20 (tag, headline, one-line subline). Monica carries about 20 to 30 in headline and subline, plus many UI labels and chips. A typical reference carries about 10 to 25 words of marketing text.

The left gutter from the panel is only about 25px. About 160px of black sits empty above the tabs. Tile: above the middle for its job. The wordmark is huge, the drop is unmistakable, and the cream ties it to the screenshots. At half size the tagline is still legible. Nothing to change.

Verdict
- Ship: yes.
- Blocking fixes: none. No claim overreaches, the sample labels are present, and nothing looks broken. The tight edges on 1, 4 and 5 are polish. 4 comes closest to looking cropped by accident, but a buyer won't misread it.

At the set level, the single 480px panel is a real gain. Swiping 1 through 5, the logo, headline and subline no longer jump sideways.

Text, frame by frame:
- 1: "A Chrome extension for YouTube that replaces the home feed with one pick." The listing is already on the Chrome Web Store, so "A Chrome extension for" says nothing. Use "Replaces YouTube's home feed with one pick." That keeps YouTube named, which matters because the tile doesn't name it. The legend lines are needed, since the capture shows both tokens with no explanation. Keep "new" in "A card reveals one new video." It's accurate, it's what a card does, and I don't think the one-reviewer cut is worth making. "Sample video." stays under the hard limit.
- 2: Every line earns its place. Nothing missing.
- 3: "Or set your own mix." is needed, because it is the only pointer to Customize. Nothing missing.
- 4: "Today stays at 4 until 4:00am." repeats the chip right under it ("4 drops until 4:00am · Undo"), and the subline already says loosening waits for the daily reset. Cut the note to "Raised to 5 drops." This was a one-reviewer ask last round; I'm the second.
- 5: Nothing extra. "Unlocks used" goes unexplained in this frame, but 2's "I need this one · 3 left this week" covers it for anyone swiping through. Not worth caption text.

On the open questions: a bigger cyclist in 1's thumbnail isn't a real problem. The figure reads fine at this size, and the thumbnail's job is "this is a video," which it does.
