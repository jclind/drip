Median design is 7.

The best ones (Adobe 08, 09 and NordPass 11, 13) make one claim per image, in 3 to 6 words, and show one real UI element large enough to read at carousel size. Usually it bleeds off an edge or overlaps a second layer (09's Edit panel over the browser, 11's dialog over the laptop), which gives depth without decoration. NordPass also keeps one system across the set: the teal, the fingerprint lines, the lime chip, and a huge headline that fills its column top to bottom. The weaker Monica ones (01, 03, 07) split the frame into panels, sprinkle sparkle emoji and 3D icons, fill the UI with AI-generated content, and run long sublines. They look templated.

Text load: a typical reference has a 4 to 8 word headline, a 8 to 15 word subline, and at most one chip or label, about 15 to 25 words in all. Adobe runs about 4 words. Monica 01 is the outlier at 60 or more. Almost no reference uses leader-line callouts. Labels sit on the UI or as pills.
