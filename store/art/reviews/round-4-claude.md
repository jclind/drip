Image 1, screenshot-1 (home, one revealed video)

What pushes the scores. Up: the caption "One video at a time." is the whole product in five words, and the panel proves it. One thumbnail, one Reveal next button with a -1 card cost, and a counter strip (34m left today, 2 drops, 7 cards) that introduces the tokens without a word of explanation. Down: the illustration is flat gradient-mountain clip art with ruler-straight rain lines. It reads as stock or generated, and it's the largest object in the first image. The "Stashed" chip shows its active state, so the first image makes the video look already saved, which raises a question the caption doesn't answer. The gap between the title and the "Trail Days · 1.2M views" row (about 75px) leaves the card looking loose.

What works. Clear hierarchy. Caption and panel say the same thing. The token row is legible at full size, and the subline is two short lines.

What to fix, by impact:
1. Illustration: give it a focal point and some texture. Angled, uneven rain instead of seven parallel strokes, a warmer light break behind the rider, a darker foreground. As drawn it's the strongest AI-look signal in the set.
2. Sample data: show the stash button unpressed (Stash, not Stashed) so the first frame reads as "a new video just arrived."
3. Crop: the panel content ends at y=748 with the panel running to the bottom edge. Scale the capture up about 5% or shift it down so the Reveal row doesn't sit near the frame edge while the counter has 100px of air above it.

Image 2, screenshot-2 (lock screen)

What pushes the scores. Up: the lock card is clean. "+15m · -1 drop" against "Done for now" makes the choice obvious, and "1h watched today" matches the 4-drop default. The caption "Out of minutes? The set

Story in the first one or two. Yes. 1 (one video, cards) and 2 (the lock) tell the whole loop. The gap is that Quiet mode (search only) never appears visually, and it's in the product description. Image 3 covers it only in small text.

Consistency. Caption position, size, and the drip mark are consistent, and that holds the set together. The panels don't match each other. Image 1 is a narrow centered column with empty sides. 2 is full bleed. 3 and 4 fill wide with different top offsets (125 vs 150) and different empty bottoms. 5 adds an inner card with its own border. Top margins inside the panel run from 16px to 140px and bottom gaps from 17px to 100px. Pick one rule (content fills the panel width minus 48px, top padding about 60px) and apply it to all five. Caption lengths are close. Image 4's line break is the odd one.

Thumbnail legibility. Captions read at thumbnail size in all five. Panels: 1 (the big thumbnail) and 2 (white button on a dark card) survive. 3 and 4 collapse into gray lists. 5 keeps the blue bars, which helps. Scaling up 3 and 4 is the main lever.

Copy flags. "Three levels. Switch any time." reads templated and contradicts the panel. "Choose the hours drops can't be used." is clumsy. "See where your time went." is generic but acceptable. Image 1's caption and subline and image 2's caption are the strongest lines.

Rejection or misleading risk. Low for branding. "YouTube's home feed" is plain descriptive use, and no YouTube logo or real thumbnail appears. The Watch button's play glyph is drip's own interface. Misleading risk: "Switch any time" against the 24-hour delay in the same image, and image 5's subline against its chart. Neither should get the listing rejected, but both are claims that don't match what the image shows.

Top 5 fixes, ranked:
1. Screenshot 2: make the rising water read as water, with a visible waterline over a recognizable paused video, larger and fewer bubbles, nothing touching the card, and the right-edge seam removed.
2. Screenshot 3 caption: replace "Switch any time." because the panel's 24-hour line contradicts it.
3. Unify panel crop and scale across 1, 3, 4, 5 (same padding rule; scale up 3 and 4; make 5's inner card fill or sit with equal margins).
4. Screenshot 1 illustration: add depth and a focal point, and make the rain uneven. Also show Stash unpressed.
5. Use tile d3 over c, with more space under the wordmark, a slightly larger tagline, and the group re-centered. Also fix screenshot 5's subline to say "the week before."
