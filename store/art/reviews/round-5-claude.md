The set

Story order: yes. Image 1 shows the core promise (one video, tokens) and image 2 shows the consequence (the lock). That's the right pair to lead. Image 2 lets it down by not showing its water visual.

Consistency: headline position, subline position, note type, and the drip mark are consistent. The inconsistencies:
- Leaders run straight in 2, 4, and 5 but angle in 1 and 3, and the angled one in 1 crosses UI.
- Panel treatment: 1, 3, and 4 sit flush in the dark strip, 2 and 5 are floating cards, and 5 has a lighter card with big margins.
- Panel fill: 4 and 5 are full, 3 leaves 100 px under Next, and 2 has empty dark space above and below the card.
- Note count: 5 has one note, the others two.
- Subline length: 2 runs three lines, the others two.
- Left-column empty space is largest in 5 and 1.

Thumbnail legibility: the serif headlines carry every frame. All notes vanish. Right panels that hold up: 1 (the illustration and "34m") and 5 (bars). Images 3 and 4 become grey rows, and 2 becomes a dark blob with one white button. Tile-c's tagline is borderline small, and tile-p reads but carries no information.

Copy flags:
- "See where the time went.", "Set the limit once.", and "When the time runs out." are stock phrases. Fine, but they push the set toward the template look.
- "Choose how much drip does." echoes the UI title.
- "Limits are optional." is a tacked-on disclaimer.
- "No watching on drops overnight." is unclear.
- "a single video you chose to see" is inaccurate.
- The notes on 3 and 5 are redundant with the on-screen text.
- The other lines read like a person wrote them, the subline in image 4 best of all.

Rejection or misleading risk: low. YouTube appears only as a descriptive word, with no logo or red play mark. The video card is fictional, and the "Watch" icon isn't the YouTube logo. Two soft spots:
- Image 2 claims water rising that the image doesn't show.
- Saturday's 1 unlock with only 2 of 4 drops used contradicts how the lock works.
Neither should cause a rejection, but both are claims that don't match the picture.
