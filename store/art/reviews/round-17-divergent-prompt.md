## The product

drip is a Chrome extension that replaces YouTube's endless home feed with a search box or one recommended video at a time, with optional daily watch-time limits. Its tokens are a blue cartoon "drop" (15 minutes of watching, by default) and a gold cartoon "card" (reveals one new video). When the minutes run out, a lock comes up over the video with blue water rising. The tone it aims for: a little character without being distracting; restraint should feel good.
