IMAGE 1 (home page, one revealed video)

To reach 9 and 2: give the left column a deliberate vertical rhythm, make the thumbnail look less like stock vector art, and drop "YouTube" as the first word of the headline (see the risk notes at the end).

Pulls the score down:
- The left column has three loose groups (headline block, legend, note) separated by uneven gaps of about 130px and 160px. The legend floats with nothing to anchor it.
- The thumbnail is a smooth gradient sky with layered flat hills and a tiny cyclist. It reads as a generic stock or generated vector scene, and that is the main thing raising the AI score.
- "YouTube" is the headline's first word.

Pushes it up:
- The note "Not this one? The next pick costs a card." is the best copy in the set. It is specific, it sounds human, and its leader lands on the Reveal next button that has the -1 card on it.
- The legend teaches both tokens in two short lines before the viewer needs them.
- The right panel is full and balanced.

What works: the token counter at the top right and the legend agree. The video meta looks real. The story is clear in about two seconds.

Fixes, by impact:
1. Headline: change it to "One video at a time." and move "for YouTube" into the subline, e.g. "A Chrome extension for YouTube. The endless home feed becomes one pick."
2. Legend: move it up to about 50px under the subline so it reads as the key to the counter, and leave the lower half to the note. Or move it down so the gaps above and below it are equal. Right now it sits in neither place.
3. Illustration: flatten or band the sky gradient, give the rain and clouds a looser, hand-drawn line, and add one specific detail, such as a trail sign or a second rider far behind. That makes it look drawn by a person instead of rendered.
4. Leader line: the horizontal run elbows down at x=830 to the dot. End the line straight on the button's left edge at the label's height so there is no elbow.

THE SET

Story in the first one or two images: yes. Frame 1 says what drip does (one pick, tokens explained), and frame 2 shows the consequence (the lock). That covers the pitch. Frame 3 might do better as frame 2 for first-time readers, but the lock is the more striking image, so the current order is the right call.

Consistency:
- The drip mark, headline position, headline size and two-line sublines match across all five. That part is solid.
- Right-panel crops don't match. The tops of the content sit at about y=40 in 1, full bleed in 2, about y=110 in 3 and 4, and y=48 in 5.
- Panel tone: frame 5 is lighter than the rest.
- Empty space: 3 and 4 leave the bottom fifth of both panels empty, and 1 has wide gaps around the legend.
- Note placement is tied to the targets, which is correct, but 4's first note crowds the subline.
- Right margins differ: about 60px in 1, about 33px in 4.

Legibility at thumbnail size: the headlines hold up. Sublines and notes don't, which is expected. The right panels read in this order: 5 (chart), 2 (lock card), 1 (video), 3 (option list), 4 (a settings list that looks like any settings page). Frame 4 depends on its headline to make sense when small.

Copy that reads as AI-written or generic:
- "When time runs out." is generic, and "runs out" repeats in the band note on the same frame.
- "Water rises as your watch time runs out." describes the picture instead of adding a fact.
- "Unlocks are "I need this one" passes, 3 a week by default." is stiff.
- "Easy to tighten. Slow to loosen." follows a parallel-slogan pattern, but it is specific enough to keep.
Everything else reads like a person wrote it.

Rejection and misleading risks:
- Frame 1's headline makes "YouTube" its subject. The Chrome Web Store's impersonation and trademark rules penalize anything that implies the product is YouTube's or is endorsed by YouTube. "One video at a time" plus "for YouTube" in the subline is safer.
- No YouTube logo or red appears anywhere. The Watch button's play glyph is generic. All the thumbnails are drawn.
- Frame 2's water band in the caption panel is decoration that doesn't exist in the product. The note explains it, so it isn't misleading.
- Frame 5 is labeled as sample data.
- I see no claims that contradict the product as described.

Top 5 fixes across the set:
1. Screenshot 2: draw one continuous water surface across both panels at the same height, and un-blur the video under the lock enough to recognize the ridge scene from screenshot 1.
2. Screenshot 4: shift or scale the page so the Drops row sits near y=430, which moves note 1 away from the subline and fills the empty bottom. Rewrite note 1 so it stops repeating the 4am rule, and match the "4:00am" format.
3. Screenshot 1: lead with "One video at a time." and move "for YouTube" into the subline, to remove the trademark-as-subject risk.
4. Screenshots 3 and 5: line up the right-panel crops so the content tops sit at about y=110 across 3, 4 and 5. Scale 3 up to fill its bottom. Bring 5's panel tone in line with the others.
5. Screenshot 5: reroute the Unlocks leader so it no longer skims the tile tops, and rewrite that note. Then pick c2 for the tile unless the c3 card is redrawn so it reads as a card.
