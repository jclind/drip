THE SET

Story in the first two: yes. 1 shows the feed replaced by one pick with the token cost. 2 shows the lock. A buyer gets the product from those two alone.

Consistency: the caption panels are consistent (same width, logo, headline position and serif). Inconsistencies:
- Strip right gutter. About 50px in 1, about 40px in 3, about 25px in 4. Fix 4.
- Strip left edge. Varies from x≈585 (4) to x≈610 (1). Minor.
- Subline measure. 3 wraps much narrower than the rest.
- Panel fill. 1, 4 and 5 all have large empty cream areas, of different sizes and in different places. That reads as restraint in 4 but as leftover space in 5.
- Leader-line elbows. Short jogs in 1 (about 19px) and 4 (about 11px) against straight lines in 2, 3 and 5. Make the elbows either clearly deliberate or gone.

Thumbnail legibility: headlines hold up at thumbnail size. Notes and sublines won't. At thumbnail size, 1 and 2 are distinctive, 3 reads as cards, and 4 and 5 look like generic dark settings and chart screens. That's fine for frames 4 and 5 of 5.

Copy flags:
- "Three ways to use drip." Listicle template.
- "Your week in minutes." Generic dashboard line.
- "Looser ones wait for the reset after day one." Awkward and ambiguous.
- Screenshot 2's subline and bottom note repeat each other.
- Strongest lines: "Easy to tighten. Slow to loosen." and "Not this one? The next pick costs a card."

Rejection or misleading risk: low. "YouTube" appears only descriptively ("for YouTube" in 1's subline, and drip's own UI in 3). There's no YouTube logo, the thumbnail is drawn, and sample data is labeled in 5. The Watch button's play glyph is black and white, not YouTube's red mark. No claim outruns the product.

Top 5 fixes across the set:
1. Screenshot 4 subline: replace "Looser ones wait for the reset after day one" with "Looser ones wait for the daily reset." Screenshot 3 already shows the first-day rule.
2. Screenshot 3 subline: fix the narrow break after "just". One line, or the shared wrap width.
3. Screenshot 4 strip: rescale so the right gutter matches the other frames (about 40 to 50px).
4. Screenshot 2 subline: stop repeating the water note. "An optional daily limit, counted in drops."
5. Screenshot 5 sample data: set Saturday's Unlocks used to 0 so a day under the limit doesn't show an unlock.
