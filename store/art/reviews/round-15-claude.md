THE SET

Story: yes. Image 1 shows the core idea, one video and a cost to skip. Image 2 shows the consequence, the lock. Someone who sees only the first two gets the product.

Consistency:
- The drip mark, headline, subline and leader color sit in the same positions across all five frames. Good.
- The strips disagree on margins. Image 4's right margin is ~25px against ~40 to 50px elsewhere. Image 3 has a large empty band at the bottom, and image 4 has one at the top.
- Leaders are mostly level with dots. The exceptions are image 1's elbow and image 3's dotless first leader.
- Caption length is consistent: two-line headlines and two-line sublines everywhere.
- Empty space in the caption panel is heaviest in images 1 and 5.

Thumbnail legibility: all five headlines hold. Notes and legend text don't, which is expected. On the right side, images 1, 2 and 5 still read as a video card, a lock and a chart. Image 3 reads as stacked cards. Image 4 reads as gray rows.

Copy flags:
- "Choose what drip does." is generic and repeats the UI heading.
- "A drop is 15 minutes, by default." sounds hedged because of the comma.
- "At zero, the water rises over the paused video." doesn't say zero what.
- Nothing reads as AI-written beyond those. "Easy to tighten. Slow to loosen." and "Not this one? The next pick costs a card." sound like a person wrote them.

Rejection or misleading risk: low.
- No YouTube logo or real thumbnails, and "for YouTube" in a subline is a normal descriptive use.
- History is labeled as a sample.
- Image 1's subline says drip replaces the feed "with one pick", which leaves out Quiet mode's search-only option. Image 3 covers that, so it isn't misleading.
- Sizes match store specs: 1280x800 and 440x280.

Top 5 fixes across the set:
1. Image 3 headline. Replace "Choose what drip does." with "Choose how strict." or another line that doesn't echo the UI's "How much should drip do?".
2. Image 5 leader. Repoint it to the "Drops used" tile, or restore the "Pick a day" copy so the note matches the date it points at.
3. Image 2 seam. Match or fade the caption band's right end into the strip's water color, and remove or enlarge the navy smudge at the strip's top-left.
4. Leader consistency. Remove image 1's elbow and give image 3's first leader a dot, so all seven leaders share one style.
5. Image 4 margins. Scale down 3 to 4 percent and shift up ~20px, so the right margin matches the other frames and the top gap shrinks.
