THE SET

Story in the first two images: yes. Image 1 says "one video at a time" and shows a cost for the next one. Image 2 shows the limit. A viewer gets the product in two images. Image 1 never shows the "before" (the endless feed), so "swaps YouTube's home feed" relies on the subline alone. That's acceptable.

Consistency:
- Crop and scale: the strip's content starts at x 595 to 620 and y 42 to 203 depending on the frame. Image 5's inset card is the outlier. Image 4 has 140px of empty black at the bottom, and image 3 about 110px.
- Leaders: elbow angles vary from flat to steep with no rule, and one dot (image 1) misses its target.
- Caption length: 2, 2, 1, 2, 2 lines, which is fine. Sublines are 2, 2, 2, 2, 1. Notes wrap with orphans in images 3 and 4.
- Left panel: the same template every time, with large empty middles. At 44% of the width, it takes a lot of room for two short notes.

Legibility at thumbnail size: captions hold up. The notes (about 18px at full size) and the leaders disappear, so the thumbnails rely on the caption and the strip. The strip in image 2 becomes a dark card on mush. Image 5's chart still reads as bars.

Copy flags:
- "drip swaps YouTube's home feed for one pick from it." Clunky ending.
- "Curated has no daily limits at all." Filler, and it repeats the UI.
- "From 11pm, minutes left go back as whole drops." Hard to parse.
- "Click a day to see what it used." Vague.
- "See where the time went." Generic screen-time phrasing. Acceptable, but every wellbeing app uses it.
- "Four drops a day." Jargon as a headline, and it repeats its own note.
The best lines are "Stricter settings apply now. Looser ones wait until tomorrow." and "Your video pauses at the daily limit. Limits are optional."

Rejection or misleading risks: no YouTube logo, red play button, or real thumbnails, so branding is fine. "YouTube" appears only as plain description. Claims match the product as described. The drawn thumbnail might suggest drip replaces thumbnails with art, but it reads clearly as sample content. Low risk.

Top 5 fixes:
1. Image 5: make the History strip full-bleed like the others, or apply one panel treatment to all five.
2. Leaders across the set: run them flat at the target's y, or use one fixed elbow angle, and put every dot on its element (image 1's Reveal next dot first).
3. Image 4: new caption built on "easy to tighten, slow to loosen", a one-line Dry hours note, and a capture scaled to fill the empty bottom.
4. Image 2: an illustration under the lock with shapes that survive the blur, and larger, fewer bubbles, so the strip reads as a paused video under water.
5. Notes that repeat the UI or wrap to an orphan (images 3, 4, 5): rewrite each to one line that says something the panel doesn't. Ship tile-c, scaled up, and drop tile-p.
