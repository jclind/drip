drip takes YouTube's home feed away.

In its place you get a search box, or one recommended video at a time. The recommendations beside the video and the end screens are gone too, and autoplay is off. Search still works, minus the shelves YouTube slips into it.

Pick how much YouTube you want when you install:

Quiet: a search box where the home feed was. No recommendations, no limits.
Curated: reveal as many recommendations as you want, one at a time. Save the ones worth watching to Stash.
Full drip: Curated with daily limits. By default you get 10 cards, each revealing one video, and 4 drops, each worth 15 minutes of watching. When the minutes run out, the video pauses.

You can switch modes or change the numbers later. Loosening a limit waits for the next day, so a late-night "just one more" can't raise it.

drip has no server, no account, and no tracking. Everything stays in your browser.
