drip

YouTube without the endless feed. You get a small allowance each day and spend it on purpose.

Two kinds of tokens

  • Watch tokens buy 15 minutes of watching each. Minutes only drain while a video plays.
  • Rec tokens reveal one recommended video at a time. You can watch it, save it for later, or pass.

You get 4 watch tokens and 10 rec tokens a day. Unspent tokens carry over for up to 2 days. Minutes you've bought end at the daily reset, 4:00am.

What changes on YouTube

  • The home page is your token page instead of a grid of videos.
  • Watch pages have no suggestions sidebar, no end screen, and no autoplay. Comments wait behind a button.
  • Shorts open as normal videos, so they count like any other.
  • Search shows 10 results at a time.
  • Search, your channels, and your playlists still work as before. Videos embedded on other sites don't use your minutes.

Start

Open YouTube and click Add 15 min before your first video. A video you open with no minutes left pauses until you add some.

When you really need one more video, the lock has an I need this one button. You type why, and it unlocks that video for the day. It's limited, and every use goes in your log.

Making limits stricter takes effect now. Loosening them waits until the next reset, so a late-night change can't undo the day.
